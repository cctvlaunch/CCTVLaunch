How to Give Access to Meta Ads

Follow our step by step guide below:

Step 1:

Go to https://business.facebook.com/settings
Ensure you're logged into the correct Facebook Business Manager.

Step 2:

Click on “Invite People”.

Step 3:

Add Our Email Address: patrick@cctvlaunch.com.au
Then Click "Next"

Step 4:

Assign us Access.
We recommend sharing full access so that we have access to all the necessary features. 
This helps us work efficiently. 

Step 5:

Select The Correct Ad Account → Select Correct "Asset".
We recommend sharing full access → Manage Ad Accounts, so that we have access to all the necessary features. 

Step 6:

Review all the information and then click "Send Invitation".

and you are done! :)
